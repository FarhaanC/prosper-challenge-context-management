# Behaviour decisions made while building the catalog and rules module (Tue 6 Oct)

These refine the plan (`03-phase-2-plan.md`). Treat them as part of the spec.

| # | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| 23 | Day and time wishes are stored as structured fields: a list of days (weekday names like "Thursday", ISO dates like "2026-10-13", or "today"/"tomorrow") plus a time of day ("morning" = before 12:00, "afternoon" = 12:00 or later). The AI converts the caller's words into these fields; code does not parse free speech. |
| 24 | "Near X" (near, close to, around, next to, not far from, by) ranks options by distance from X but does not exclude other places. "The X one" / "X" naming one of our locations restricts options to that location. A name that fits several of our locations (e.g. "Mission" fits Mission Bay and Mission District; "North" fits North Beach and North Gate) must not be guessed: the result says it's ambiguous so the agent asks. Unknown places are reported, not guessed. |
| 25 | Rule 6 (doctor at several sites: settle the location) is enforced by design: every offered option is doctor + location + time together, and the final check refuses a booking with no location. In the fake schedule a doctor works at exactly one of their locations on any given weekday, so they can't be double-booked across sites. |
| 26 | When the caller changes one choice, the old doctor/location become wishes (preferred_provider_id / preferred_location_id), not choices. The next search honours them if they still fit and adds a plain note if they don't. Nothing is silently kept or dropped. The change table: visit type change clears specialty, visit type, doctor, location, time and goes to stage "visit_type"; doctor change clears doctor, location, time (old location kept as a wish) and goes to "booking"; location change clears doctor, location, time (old doctor kept as a wish) and goes to "booking"; time change clears doctor, location, time (both kept as wishes, new days/time-of-day recorded) and goes to "booking". If no visit type is chosen yet, doctor/location/time changes go to "visit_type". |
| 27 | "New patient" means new to the clinic group, not new to that doctor. A returning patient may book a doctor who isn't accepting new patients. |
| 28 | A referral is stored as a specialty name and covers every referral-needing visit type in that specialty. |
| 29 | Fake patient lookup needs first AND last name to match (forgivingly, misheard spellings allowed); a surname alone does not identify a patient; a date of birth separates two patients with the same name. Anyone not found is a new patient with no referrals. |

## Fake data the module adds (described in the plan's "What we fake")

- 6 fake returning patients in `data/patients.json`.
- Fake coordinates and neighbourhoods for the 8 locations plus well-known San Francisco places in `data/location_extras.json`.
- Fake open slots: generated by code, repeatable (same inputs give the same answer every run), within opening hours (Mon-Fri 8:00-17:00), lunch 12:00-13:00 blocked, 30-minute blocks, about 14 days ahead, at least 2 hours' notice, a visit needs enough consecutive free blocks for its duration and must end by closing time. Bookings made through the agent remove that time from what's offered (no double booking).
- Options: at most 3 per search, all already valid for this caller; spread across different doctor/location pairs before repeating one.
